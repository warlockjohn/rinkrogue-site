Privacy Policy

Updated: November 21, 2026

In short: the game does not collect your personal data. No sign-up, no ads, no third-party analytics. Your progress stays on your phone.

What is stored on your phone

Your career save, unlocked teams and rinks, trophies on the shelf, sound and language settings, and which tips you have seen. All of it lives inside the app on your iPhone; the developer has no access to it.

Match statistics in test versions

Test versions of the game may send the developer anonymous statistics about finished matches to help tune the balance: time of the match, your team and the opponent, tournament stage, score, number of shots, penalties and fights, and which cards were played. It contains no name, email, phone number, advertising identifier, location or anything else that could identify a player. The data is kept in the developer's spreadsheet (Google) and used only to tune the game.

Internet and third-party services

The game works without an account. It uses the internet to download game updates (Expo) and, in test versions, to send the statistics described above. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location. Vibration is used only for in-match effects.

TestFlight

If you test the beta, Apple may share crash reports and your feedback with the developer, including whatever you choose to put in it. See the TestFlight privacy terms.

Children

The game is not designed to collect data about anyone, including children, and never asks for personal information.

Deleting data

Delete the app and all progress is removed from your phone. Anonymous statistics cannot be linked to a particular player, so it is not possible to find and delete "your" records; if you have questions, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
