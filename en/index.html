Pick a team, build a deck — win the neighborhood cup

A card roguelike about backyard hockey. Pick a team — from retired ice-swimmers to the grannies from the porch bench — build your deck and fight through the tournament: group stage, playoffs, final and a boss. Pass, shoot, throw your weight around by the boards, and if time runs out — it's a shootout.

What's inside

Six teams, six personalities

Backyard Crew, Old Walruses, The Foamers, Street Rats, Scout Leaders and Porch Grannies. Each has its own deck and its own signature move.

A match is a game of cards

Three periods of four turns. Every card is an action: pass, shot, check, save. You never have the legs for everything — and late on you can pull the goalie.

From the yard to the mountain top

Three acts: the yard, the forest and a snowy summit, each with its own boss. Between matches — the Flea Market, the Sauna, the Coach and chests.

Stickers and trophies

Achievements unlock new cards and talismans, and every rink you beat puts a shinier trophy on your shelf.
